how do I install a Chinese voice?
  • Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Manage voices → Add voices → Chinese (Simplified, China). Restart the browser. Microsoft Edge also has good built-in online Chinese voices.
  • Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices → Chinese (China mainland) → download Tingting. Restart the browser.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese (China mainland) → download a voice.
  • Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → Google → Install voice data → Chinese (Mandarin).
  • Chromebook: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → Speech engines/voices → add Chinese.
  • Chrome on desktop usually comes with "Google 普通话" built in. If this page is open inside an app or preview pane, open it directly in Chrome or Edge.

The online option sends the sentence you're playing to Google Translate's speech service to get the audio. Nothing else is sent.

A 10-day plan, about 45–60 min a day. Days 1–5 go through the content topic by topic, Days 6–7 prepare you for the unit test, and Days 8–10 prepare you for the exam. Every day starts with flashcards, because a few minutes of spaced review each day sticks much better than one long session. Tick tasks off as you go; your progress is saved in this browser.

Fewer days? Compressed versions
  • 5 days: Day 1 = Days 1+2 · Day 2 = Days 3+4 · Day 3 = Day 5 + Day 6 · Day 4 = Days 7+8 · Day 5 = Days 9+10.
  • 2–3 days: (1) flashcards on all decks in 'Weak only' mode + Grammar tab; (2) oral drill on the Unit test pool + listening mocks; (3) reading + translation mock + one timed writing piece.
  • Unit test first? Do Days 1–5 flashcards and then go straight to Days 6–7. Leave Days 8–10 for after the unit test.

On the day

  • Oral: if you blank, repeat the question as a statement to buy time (我最喜欢的科目是……), then give a reason with 因为.
  • Listening: on the first play, write down numbers, prices and names. On the second play, fill in the details.
  • Reading: read the questions first, then find the paragraph each one comes from. The questions are in the same order as the text.
  • Translation: translate the meaning, not word by word (三层楼 → "three-storey building"). Never leave a gap; a sensible guess can still earn marks.
  • Writing: plan for 3 min, write for 15, check characters for 2.

Flashcards

Grammar

Every one of these appears in your sample texts. Markers give credit for using a variety of patterns, so aim to get 5–6 of them into each piece of writing.

1 一……就…… as soon as … (then) …
你一进学校大门就可以看见图书馆。
As soon as you enter the school gate you can see the library.
一 and 就 both go after the subject(s): 我一回家就做作业。
2 因为……所以…… because … so …
因为我对中文感兴趣，所以我要学中文。
Because I'm interested in Chinese, I want to study it.
You can drop one half, but using both looks good in writing.
3 除了……以外，……还…… apart from …, also …
除了数学课以外，我还喜欢上生物课。
Apart from maths, I also like biology.
With 都 it means 'except': 除了历史以外，我都喜欢 = I like everything except history.
4 对……感兴趣 be interested in …
我对历史和地理很感兴趣。
I'm very interested in history and geography.
很 goes before 感兴趣. Negative: 我对历史不感兴趣。
5 A 比 B + adj A is more … than B
菜市场的水果比超市便宜。
Fruit at the market is cheaper than at the supermarket.
Never put 很 after 比. Use 要 / 一点儿 / 多了: 比猪肉要贵, 比超市便宜多了。
6 Price: 价钱 + 一斤 … per jin
活鱼三十块五一斤。／一斤葡萄要十块五毛。
Live fish is 30.50 a jin. / A jin of grapes costs 10.50.
块 = yuan, 毛 = 10 cents. 一百多块 = over 100 yuan.
7 可以……吗？ asking permission
我可以借你的英语课本吗？
Can I borrow your English textbook?
Also: 我现在看电视，可以吗？
8 忘记 / 忘了 + verb (+了) forgot to …
我今天忘记带铅笔了。
I forgot to bring my pencil today.
The 了 at the end shows it has happened.
9 向 / 跟 + person + 借 + thing borrow from someone
我向同学借了一本练习本。
I borrowed an exercise book from a classmate.
借给 + person = lend to: 我借给他一支笔。
10 Result complements: V + 完/好/见/到/光/饱 shows the result of the action
写完作业 · 做好 · 看见 · 找到 · 卖光 · 吃饱
finish homework · get done · see · find · sell out · eat full
Can't-do form: 吃不饱 (can't get full), 看不见 (can't see).
11 V 完……以后 after finishing …
我每天晚上写完作业以后都听音乐。
Every evening after I finish my homework I listen to music.
Also plain ……以后: 午饭以后 / 放学以后.
12 从小就…… since (I was) little
我从小就喜欢吃面条。
I've liked noodles since I was little.
13 每次……都…… every time …
姐姐每次点菜都点烤鸭。
Every time my sister orders she gets roast duck.
Same with 每天都 / 每个星期都.
14 应该多 / 少 + V should … more / less
我们应该多吃蔬菜，少吃糖。
We should eat more vegetables and less sugar.
多/少 go BEFORE the verb.
15 V 得 + adj describing how something is done
她每次都吃得很饱。
She always eats till she's very full.
我中文说得不太好。
16 ……对……好 / 不好 good / bad for …
糖吃多了对牙齿不好。
Too much sugar is bad for your teeth.
对身体不好 = bad for your health.
17 Location: A 在 B 的 旁边 / 隔壁 / 对面 / 前面 A is beside / next door to / opposite / in front of B
图书馆在教学楼的隔壁。
The library is next door to the teaching building.
Existence: 学校有一个图书馆。Floors: 我的教室在二楼。
18 Frequency & time words 常常 · 经常 · 平时 · 通常 · 一般 · 总是 · 有时候
我平时喜欢吃鱼柳饭，但是周末通常吃炸鸡。
Normally I like fish fillet rice, but on weekends I usually have fried chicken.
They go after the subject, before the verb. Negative of 常常 is 不常.
19 还是 vs 或者 'or'
你喜欢铅笔还是钢笔？／我去图书馆或者操场。
Pencil or pen? (question) / I go to the library or the field. (statement)
还是 = questions, 或者 = statements.
20 只好 have no choice but to
我忘了带练习本，只好向同学借了一本。
I forgot my exercise book, so I had to borrow one.
21 而且 / 也 and also / moreover
菜市场的蔬菜很新鲜，而且比超市便宜。
The vegetables are fresh, and cheaper too.
Better than 和 for joining clauses (和 only joins nouns).
22 Approximate numbers two neighbouring numbers
我今年十五六岁。／一个星期三四次
I'm 15 or 16. / 3 or 4 times a week
No 或 needed between the numbers.
23 再来 + measure + dish one more …
再来一个红烧豆腐。
One more braised tofu, please.
再 = again / another.
24 让 + person + V make / let someone do
老师让我们写汉字。
The teacher had us write characters.

Easy-to-confuse words & tones

一 tone change一 is yí before a 4th tone (一辆 yí liàng, 一件 yí jiàn, 一位 yí wèi, 一共 yígòng) and yì before tones 1–3 (一只 yì zhī, 一条 yì tiáo, 一本 yì běn).
只 zhī vs 只 zhǐzhī = measure word (一只狗, 半只烤鸡). zhǐ = only (只好, 只能).
还 huán vs 还 háihuán = return something (还书). hái = also / still (我还喜欢……).
卷 juǎn vs juànjuǎn = to roll (卷笔刀). juàn = exam paper (试卷).
支 / 只 / 张支 zhī for pens; 只 zhī for animals; 张 zhāng for flat things like paper, tables, cards (一张卡片).
二楼In China the ground floor is 一楼, so 二楼 is one level up. Your list says 'second floor', so use that in the test.
常常 / 经常 / 平常常常 and 经常 both mean 'often'. 平常 means 'usually / ordinary', the same as 平时. It isn't about frequency.
游泳馆 vs 游泳池游泳馆 = swimming centre (the building). 游泳池 yóuyǒngchí = the pool itself.

Oral

The unit test oral is out of 20. Answer in full sentences, give a reason (因为……), and add one extra detail (也 / 而且 / 除了……以外). Swap in your own facts wherever the models don't fit you.

Random question drill

Prepared talks: memorise these

1. Subjects of Study

我星期一到星期五每天都有六节课。我最喜欢数学和科学，因为我觉得很有意思，也很有用。我也喜欢音乐，因为我喜欢唱歌和弹钢琴。可是，我不太喜欢历史，因为我觉得很难，也有很多东西要记。我每天都要学习不同的科目，比如英语、中文、体育和美术。

2. School Facilities

我的学校很大，有很多设施。学校有一个图书馆，在教学楼旁边。我经常去图书馆看书和做作业。学校还有一个体育馆，在操场旁边。我们可以在那里打篮球、打羽毛球和做运动。我的教室在二楼，教室里有很多桌子、椅子和一台电脑。

3. Stationery

每天上学的时候，我都会带很多文具。我书包里有铅笔、橡皮、尺子和练习本。我也带课本，因为上课的时候要看书。有时候我会忘记带东西，例如铅笔或钢笔。如果同学有多余的文具，我可以借他的。我的文具很重要，因为它们可以帮助我学习。

Writing

Marking criteria checklist (/20)

Content (5)Cover every bullet point in the prompt and add your own ideas or opinions (我觉得……).
Vocabulary (5)Use topic words from school, study and food. Avoid using the same word over and over; switch between 常常, 经常, 平时 and 一般.
Grammar (5)Include at least 5 patterns from the Grammar tab, e.g. 因为…所以, 除了…以外还, 比, 一…就, 从小就, 应该多/少, V完…以后.
Characters (5)Write the characters correctly. If you aren't sure of a character, use a simpler word you can write rather than pinyin.

How to plan a piece in 3 minutes

  1. Write one heading per bullet point. Each one becomes 2–3 sentences.
  2. Next to each heading, write the 1–2 grammar patterns you'll use for it.
  3. Opening line: 我今年十五岁，是一名九年级的学生。 Closing line: 我觉得…… / 我很喜欢……，因为……
  4. Count your characters. 10 sentences of about 12 characters gets you to around 120.

Model: School life (100–120 characters)

Prompt: Your daily timetable, the stationery you use every day, the places at school you often go to and where they are.

Write your own first, then open the model (115 characters)

我星期一到星期五每天都有六节课，上午三节，下午三节。我最喜欢数学课，因为我觉得数学很有意思，也很有用。除了数学以外，我还对化学和物理感兴趣。我每天都带文具盒，里面有铅笔、橡皮、尺子和卷笔刀。午饭以后，我常常去图书馆看书。图书馆在教学楼旁边。我也经常去操场踢足球。

Patterns used: ……到……每天都 · 因为……所以/也 · 除了……以外，还…… · 对……感兴趣 · ……的时候 · ……以后 · A 在 B 旁边 · 常常 / 经常

Model: Food and eating habits (100–150 characters)

Prompt: Food you like and dislike, what you usually eat, where you buy food, and a price comparison between places.

Write your own first, then open the model (144 characters)

我从小就喜欢吃中国菜，特别是蛋炒饭和红烧鱼。我不太喜欢吃薯条，因为炸的东西对身体不好。平时我早饭吃面包和煎蛋，午饭吃盒饭，晚饭跟家人一起吃米饭和青菜。周末我们总是去菜市场买菜，因为菜市场的蔬菜很新鲜，而且比超市便宜。比如，在菜市场一斤葡萄要十块，在超市要二十块。活鱼三十块一斤，比猪肉贵。我觉得我们应该多吃蔬菜，少吃糖果。

Patterns used: 从小就 · 不太 · 因为…… · 平时 · 总是 · 而且 · A 比 B 便宜/贵 · 比如 · price: 十块一斤 · 应该多……少……
